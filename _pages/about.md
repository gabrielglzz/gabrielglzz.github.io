I'm an Assistant Professor (_Profesor Ayudante Doctor_) at the **Universidad de Castilla-La Mancha (UCLM)**, where I teach in the Computer Engineering degree at the Talavera de la Reina campus. These days you'll find me explaining how a processor really works, from pipelines and caches in **Computer Architecture** and **Computer Structure** to the protocols that keep data moving in the **Computer Networks** labs. I also teach **Microprocessor Architecture** in UCLM's PERTE Chip Master's programme. I've been in front of a classroom since 2021, and it's still the part of my job I enjoy most. That's also why I build my own teaching tools, like an [interactive RISC-V pipeline simulator](https://ggomezlo.es/riscv-fp-pipeline/) that helps students see, cycle by cycle, what the textbook describes.

My research lives in the world of **High-Performance Computing (HPC)** and data centers, more precisely in the interconnection networks that let thousands of nodes work as one machine. I've always been drawn to the gap between how a network is designed on paper and how it behaves once it's deployed, and that's where most of my work sits. I deployed the first known **KNS topology** on a real InfiniBand cluster (CELLIA), worked on **congestion control** mechanisms driven by fine-grain network monitoring, and extended the **SAURON** simulator to model next-generation architectures such as **BXIv3**, integrating it with **COSSIM** to simulate RISC-V and ARM systems within the European **RED-SEA project**.

I did all of this at the **Albacete Research Institute of Informatics (I3A)**, as part of the **RAAP** group, where I'm still a member. I studied Computer Engineering at UCLM (Bachelor's in 2020, Master's in 2021) and completed my **PhD in Advanced Information Technologies** there in December 2025, graded _Cum Laude_. Along the way, I had the chance to spend time at **CERN**, modeling the data acquisition network of the ATLAS experiment, and at **FORTH** in Heraklion, working on advanced congestion control. After my PhD, I joined the **Universitat Politècnica de València** as a postdoctoral researcher before returning to UCLM to teach.

If you'd like to talk about networks, computer architecture, or teaching, feel free to reach out.
